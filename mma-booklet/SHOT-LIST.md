# APEX PREDATOR MMA — Photography Shot List

20 frames. Shoot each one, then save it into `photos/` named after its slot code (for example `photos/hero-01.jpg`) and re-run `python3 build_mma_booklet.py`. Images are cropped to fit automatically, so the exact aspect ratio does not matter.

Any frame you have not shot yet appears in the booklet as a labelled placeholder showing this brief, so the layout stays finished while you work through the list.

| # | Save as | Frame | Brief |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | `photos/hero-01.jpg` | **HERO / COVER** | Fighter mid-roundhouse on the pads, shot from low and slightly behind, hard rim light from a window or a single LED panel, gym dark behind. Underexpose the background by 2 stops. |
| 2 | `photos/strip-01.jpg` | **HAND WRAPS** | Tight crop on hands being wrapped - the ritual before training. Side light, shallow depth of field. |

| 4 | `photos/strip-03.jpg` | **MAT TEXTURE** | Overhead of the tatami seams and chalk-dusted mat, feet just entering frame. |
| 5 | `photos/gym-01.jpg` | **THE ROOM** | Wide of the whole gym mid-class, bodies blurred by a slow shutter (1/30s) so the room reads busy and alive. |
| 6 | `photos/def-01.jpg` | **GRAPPLING** | Two athletes in a clinch or a guard pass. Get low, fill the frame, keep both faces partly visible. |
| 7 | `photos/def-02.jpg` | **STRIKING** | Pad work with sweat spray caught in the light. Fast shutter (1/500s) and a high ISO. |

## Consent

Get written permission from the gym owner and from every person who appears in a frame before shooting, and keep it with the files. Do not photograph anyone mid-injury.
